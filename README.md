# ITDEV-117-Individual-Project
Personal Budget Tracker
Many people struggle to keep track of their income and expenses, which can lead to overspending and financial stress.
The app is designed for students, young adults, and anyone who wants to manage their personal finances more effectively.
If you go over your budget it will send you an error and will not work because it is over your budget.
It allows people not to spend that much on a budget.
